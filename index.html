Capstone

4Ps Graduate Monitoring and Reporting System

Built for the Cabanatuan City local government’s newly created 4Ps graduates program.

Graduate records were scattered across separate spreadsheets and paper documents, which made it hard to coordinate with partner agencies and slowed down monitoring reports. We built one centralized system to replace them.

  • Graduate profiles
  • Service histories
  • Search and monitoring
  • Partner agency service records
  • Dashboards and reports

We followed the Modified Waterfall Model and guided the database design with the Database Life Cycle.

How it was rated

Four-point scale. Both overall scores were interpreted as “Very Functional”.

  • Overall, IT experts3.28
  • Overall, end-users3.40
  • Record management, search, and partner agency features3.63
  • Dashboard, reporting, usability, and notifications3.33
  • Maintainability and security3.21

Users asked for a simpler interface, clearer dashboards, more visible service-confirmation steps, and more notifications. Maintainability and security scored lowest, so they are the next areas to improve.
